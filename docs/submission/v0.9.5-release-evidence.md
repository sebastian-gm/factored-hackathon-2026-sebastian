## What judges see

Fresh reviewer sessions default to English for login, the profile picker,
navigation, controls, notices, Why rules, Agent Desk and Insights. Existing
explicit language preferences remain selectable. Customer conversations and
prepared messages retain the authenticated profile's Spanish or Portuguese.

English Desk summaries, questions and next steps come from reason IDs and
structured context. Original API summaries are explicitly labeled
**API summary (original, ES/PT)**; they are service text, not customer words.
Unknown reasons receive conservative English review guidance. This presentation
uses no model translation and grants no action authority.

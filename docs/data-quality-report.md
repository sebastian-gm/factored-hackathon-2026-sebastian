## Duplicate reconciliation

Exact records include their source primary key. Payload fingerprints omit the ID and partition date to detect replay with regenerated IDs. Business/event fingerprints are candidate collisions, not proof of duplicate activity; no records are collapsed. Counts report excess rows beyond the first member of each group. Unique PKs logically imply zero exact full-row duplicates.

See [delivery reconciliation](data-quality-reconciliation.md) for the direct-source scan, full 13-table scope, fingerprint keys, null denominators, additional relationships and volume explanations.

## Definitions

- Silver checks every row against contract types, nullability, enums and ranges. Invalid rows are quarantined; primary keys and foreign keys are checked over the complete snapshot.
- Windows are half-open UTC intervals `[BANK_CLOCK - N days, BANK_CLOCK)`. The bank business date is timestamp minus six hours, converted to date.
- Candidate counts include every customer, including zero-transaction customers. Quantiles use continuous interpolation.
- Future dimension updates compare their UTC timestamp to BANK_CLOCK. The initial report compared calendar dates to the last source business date; these definitions differ.
- All source checksums are compared on every run, including the last seven business days and older partitions. Unchanged source transformations are reused. Full gold models rebuild when a source, contract, transformation or clock changes.
- Silver Parquet objects retain source provenance. Snapshot views add the active `_dataset_version`; gold persists it on each row. Aggregate lineage uses the snapshot manifest hash and an aggregate source label.

## Interpretation of the measured results

Complaints account for 17.1% of contacts and 23.1% of handle time, with FCR 43.6%. This supports prioritizing grounded charge explanations and dispute intake.

At fraud-score > 27, the flag rate is 8.0464% over all transactions and 8.0278% in the serving window. The denominator includes null-score rows; this differs from a percentile over scored rows.

At fraud-score > 30, the flag rate is 0.0536% over all transactions and 0.0516% in the serving window. The denominator includes null-score rows; this differs from a percentile over scored rows.

App-error events were followed by contact at 0.858%, compared with 0.849% for other selected events: a 0.0095 percentage-point difference. This small descriptive gap does not support building error-triggered contact automation from this dataset.

The used-accent FCR difference (matched minus mismatched) is 0.093 percentage points; this descriptive result provides no useful basis for accent routing.

The native-accent FCR difference (matched minus mismatched) is 0.040 percentage points; this descriptive result provides no useful basis for accent routing.

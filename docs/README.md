# Start here

Aclara helps customers understand a charge or request a dispute in Spanish and
Portuguese. These six pages explain the demo and its evidence.

- [How Aclara works](architecture.md) — AI understands language; code controls access and bank actions.
- [Final evaluation (v4)](evaluation/final-v4-results.md) — Results against the rules-only baseline, including failed safety checks.
- [Safety findings](evaluation/final-v4-safety-analysis.md) — What went wrong and what the recorded evidence can explain.
- [Data quality](data-quality-report.md) — Checks, warnings and fields excluded from use.
- [Privacy and fairness](responsible-ai.md) — Privacy safeguards, language comparisons and human-review limits.
- [Run it locally](../README.md) — Start the demo without organizer data or paid-model credentials.

The pages begin with three lines; detailed evidence is one click deeper.
[Production work](production-readiness.md), [language-model limits](ml/model-card.md)
and [fresh-checkout verification](submission/clean-clone-reproduction.md) give more detail.

[Development history](history/README.md) keeps earlier evaluations, studies and
release records separate. Measurements after the final evaluation do not change
the official v4 scores.

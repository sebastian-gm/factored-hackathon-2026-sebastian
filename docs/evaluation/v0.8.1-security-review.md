Reviewed `aeeb452a0e83e300d46993aff3716e445034c85c`, including the
frontend integration. The original delegated-access defect is corrected:

- Every invitation redemption, queue read and claim validates the originating
  controller against the current judge configuration and dataset binding.

  logout fail closed. Cached claim replay does not bypass validation.

  separately signed-in staff session gets masked queue access, not banking,

  claim columns; the constrained owner function publishes an owned packet.
  Realm settings are transaction-local and cleared before connection reuse.
- A concurrent claim race produces one winner and one audited change. An
  independent read verifies the receipt. Later packet refresh preserves the
  claimant and advances the version.

The lead accepts the corrected security boundary based on authored mock and
disposable Postgres regressions. Azure migration and live queue checks are still
pending. Judge activation remains OFF; live judge-visit proof belongs to Gate B.

## Monetary-input fix — #149 corrected review accepted

The pre-existing v0.7/v0.8 product defect masked large monetary input before
NLU. The exact formatter is `f'{amount:.2f} {currency}'`; the authored
`1000000.00 COP` reproduces it without organizer values.

The first #149 head preserves that input, but zero-cost authored replay found
two regressions. `Mi teléfono de contacto es 300.123.456` and
`Contacta al 300.123.456` escape masking and become monetary evidence.
`Veo dos cargos de 125 y 234 pesos.` overwrites an extracted 125 with 234.
Corrected head `436627d` passes those two replays. A further authored privacy
replay still sends `Mi teléfono personal es 300.123.456 COP`,
`Meu telefone pessoal é 300.123.456 BRL` and
`Mi DNI termina por 123456789` unmasked and turns them into amount slots.
Ordinary intervening words bypass the fixed label list, while currency or bare
`por` grants the exemption. The lead withholds security approval for that head.

The orchestrator directed the AI owner to restore privacy-first digit redaction
and recover unambiguous money locally from raw text, without granting a provider
exemption. Identifier cues or multiple monetary amounts must remain unresolved.
Corrected head `806f4cdadc1cc14bb6d9098ae5c6cafb58170c6c` removes all
monetary redaction exemptions. The original digit masking remains unconditional;
additional masking covers punctuated documents with intervening descriptors.
Raw monetary inspection happens locally after NLU, never in a substituted
provider payload. One qualified amount without identifier cues is recovered;
identifier-bearing clauses or competing amounts clear even a model guess and
request clarification. Shared-unit lists remain unresolved. This intentionally
prefers clarification when identifier context makes an amount uncertain.

Lead-run authored payload checks cover every reported leak, the exact
`1000000.00 COP` formatter, ES/PT identifiers, shared units and regional monetary
forms. They passed alongside delegation tests and the full combined mock and
Postgres gates. The lead accepts the corrected privacy boundary.

The source head's browser workflow failed a mobile choice-card viewport check
after 162 fixture passes. The combined frontend has the additional guide/staff
changes; its prior 171-fixture run passed. The integration must pass its own
complete remote browser gate; no failed source run is described as green.
The lead repeated that exact mobile test three times on the combined production
build: **3/3 passed** without changing product code or the assertion.
